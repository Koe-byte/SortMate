# SortMate household

Students use SortMate to share household expenses, divide chores and see when roommates are home or on campus.

## Language

**Household:** The group of roommates sharing one home, expense list, chore board and quiet-hours schedule. A **join code** is the six-character invitation to that group.

**Member:** A person who belongs to a household. A member can be a bill's payer, a participant in its split or the assignee of a chore.

**Bill:** A shared expense with one payer and one or more participants. A receipt is optional. Settling a bill marks it as handled; it does not transfer money.

**Split:** The amount each participant owes toward a bill. An equal split distributes any remaining cents so all shares add up to the recorded total.

**Chore:** A household task that may have an assignee and a due date. A member can mark it complete or reopen it.

**Quiet hours:** The household's agreed study or rest window. It can span midnight. Members choose whether to receive a reminder when it begins.

**Presence:** A member's displayed status: **At home**, **On campus** or **Away**. It can change automatically or be set manually.

**Geofence:** A circular boundary around the household's home or campus. Entering or leaving one can change a member's presence.

**Outbox:** Household changes saved on one phone and still awaiting acknowledgment from the shared service.

**Demo household:** A seeded household belonging only to the presentation phone. Its edits are not shared with cloud households.

**5A evaluation:** The five field measurements in the proposal: receipt-total accuracy, boundary-to-badge delay, cross-device sync delay, four-hour battery use and recovery of five offline entries.
